Privacy Policy

Myconomy, a mushroom farming and cooking game for Android

Last updated 29 September 2026

In short: Myconomy doesn't collect any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game doesn't send anything over the internet. Your progress stays on your phone.

Who makes Myconomy

Myconomy is made by DylanD. In this policy, "we" means DylanD.

What the game stores

Your progress, such as your farm, money, recipes and settings, is saved on your device in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us, and we can't see it. If you uninstall Myconomy or clear its data, your progress is deleted.

If you use Android's own backup, Android may include this saved progress in your device backup to your Google Account, as it does for other apps. Google handles that backup, and we can't access it.

Purchases

Myconomy may offer optional packs as in-app purchases. They are bought through Google Play, which handles your payment under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. To unlock the packs you own, including on a new phone, the game asks Google Play on your device which packs your account has bought; that answer is used on your device only. Google Play gives us basic records of each sale, such as the order number, date, item and price, which we use only for accounting, refunds and support.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and doesn't use your advertising ID. Its fonts and artwork are built in, so it doesn't load anything from the internet. It doesn't ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Android and Google Play may send Google crash and performance reports about your apps, including this one. Google shows us only summaries of those reports that don't identify you.

Feedback in test versions

Test versions of Myconomy sometimes ask what you think. If you choose to answer, the game opens your own email app with a message to us already written: your answers, the game's version, your farm's day and level, and your phone's model. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and we use what you send only to improve the game.

Children

Myconomy is meant for players aged 13 and over. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
